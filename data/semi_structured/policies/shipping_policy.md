# Shipping Policy

## Processing Time

Orders are processed within 1 business day of payment confirmation. Orders placed on weekends or public holidays are processed on the next business day.

## Shipping Methods and Delivery Times

| Method            | Estimated Delivery | Cost                          |
|-------------------|---------------------|--------------------------------|
| Standard Shipping | 3–5 business days   | Free on orders over $75; otherwise $6.99 |
| Express Shipping  | 1–2 business days   | $14.99                        |

Delivery times are estimates and start counting from the date of shipment, not the date of order.

## Order Tracking

Once an order ships, a confirmation email containing a tracking number and carrier link is sent automatically. Tracking information may take up to 24 hours to become active.

## Shipping Coverage

TechCart currently ships to addresses within the country of operation only. We do not offer international shipping at this time.

## Delayed or Lost Shipments

If a shipment appears delayed beyond the estimated delivery window, or if tracking shows no movement for more than 5 business days, contact customer support with the order number for investigation.

## Shipping Damage

If a package arrives visibly damaged, do not discard the packaging. Take photos of the damage and contact support within 48 hours of delivery to initiate a claim.
