# Warranty Policy

## Standard Coverage

All new products sold on TechCart are covered by the manufacturer's standard warranty, starting from the delivery date. Warranty duration varies by product category and manufacturer:

| Category      | Typical Warranty Duration |
|---------------|----------------------------|
| GPUs          | 2–3 years (manufacturer-dependent) |
| CPUs          | 3 years (manufacturer-dependent)   |
| Accessories   | 1 year                     |

Exact warranty duration for a specific product is listed on its product page.

## What Is Covered

The manufacturer's warranty covers defects in materials or workmanship under normal use. This includes component failure not caused by external damage, misuse, or unauthorized modification.

## What Is Not Covered

- Physical damage (drops, liquid damage, burns).
- Damage caused by overclocking beyond manufacturer specifications.
- Damage from improper installation (e.g., incorrect power connector, physical force).
- Cosmetic wear that does not affect functionality.
- Products with tampered or removed warranty seals.

## How to Make a Warranty Claim

1. Contact TechCart support with your order number, a description of the issue, and photos or videos if applicable.
2. Support will determine whether the issue is covered and whether the claim should be handled directly by TechCart or forwarded to the manufacturer.
3. Depending on the manufacturer's process, you may be asked to ship the item to TechCart or directly to an authorized service center.

## Claim Resolution

Approved warranty claims are resolved by repair, replacement, or store credit, at TechCart's discretion, depending on part availability and manufacturer policy. Typical resolution time is 10–20 business days from the day the item is received for inspection.
