# About TechCart

## Who We Are

TechCart is an online retailer specializing in computer hardware and components — GPUs, CPUs, and related accessories — for gamers, PC builders, and professionals.

## What We Sell

- Graphics cards (GPUs) from major manufacturers (NVIDIA, AMD).
- Processors (CPUs), including Intel product lines.
- Compatible accessories and components.

## Customer Support

- **Availability:** Monday to Friday, 9:00 AM – 6:00 PM (store's local time zone).
- **Channels:** Live chat (this assistant), email support, and a help center with self-service articles.
- **Response time:** Live chat is answered instantly during business hours; email inquiries are answered within 1 business day.

## Store Values

- Transparent pricing and real-time stock accuracy.
- Genuine, manufacturer-sealed products only.
- Clear, predictable shipping, return, and warranty policies.

## Assistant Scope

The TechCart assistant can help with:
- Finding products by specification, price range, or brand.
- Comparing two or more products.
- Checking real-time stock availability.
- Answering questions about shipping, returns, warranty, and payment, based on the store's official policies.

The assistant does not process payments, cancel orders, or access personal account information — those actions require contacting human customer support.
