# TechCart — Frequently Asked Questions

## Ordering

**Do I need an account to place an order?**
No. TechCart supports guest checkout — you can browse, add items to your cart, and complete a purchase without creating an account.

**Can I modify or cancel an order after placing it?**
Orders can be modified or cancelled within 1 hour of purchase, as long as the order has not yet entered the "Processing" stage. Contact customer support immediately if you need to make a change.

**How do I know if an item is in stock?**
Each product page displays real-time stock availability. If an item shows "In stock," it is available for immediate shipping.

## Payment

**What payment methods are accepted?**
TechCart accepts major credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express), as well as PayPal.

**Is my payment information secure?**
Yes. All transactions are encrypted and processed through PCI-DSS compliant payment providers. TechCart never stores your full card number.

**Why was my payment declined?**
Common reasons include insufficient funds, incorrect card details, or a bank security flag on international purchases. Contact your bank first; if the issue persists, reach out to TechCart support.

## Shipping

**How long does shipping take?**
Standard shipping takes 3–5 business days. Express shipping (where available) takes 1–2 business days. See the Shipping Policy document for full details.

**Do you ship internationally?**
Currently, TechCart ships within the country of operation only. International shipping is not yet supported.

**Can I track my order?**
Yes. Once your order ships, you will receive a tracking number by email.

## Returns & Warranty

**What is your return policy?**
Most items can be returned within 30 days of delivery, provided they are unused and in original packaging. See the Return Policy document for exclusions and full conditions.

**Are products covered by warranty?**
Yes. All products sold on TechCart include the manufacturer's standard warranty. See the Warranty Policy document for details on coverage and claims.

## Products

**Are the products new or refurbished?**
Unless explicitly stated otherwise on the product page, all items sold on TechCart are brand new, sealed in original manufacturer packaging.

**Can you help me choose between two components?**
Yes — the TechCart assistant can compare specifications, price, and stock availability between products to help you decide.
